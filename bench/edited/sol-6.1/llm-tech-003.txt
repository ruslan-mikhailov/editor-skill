Kubernetes automates the deployment, scaling, and management of containerized applications across a cluster of machines. It is an open-source container orchestration platform, originally designed at Google and now a Cloud Native Computing Foundation project.

You declare the desired state in configuration files: how many replicas of a service should run, which container images to use, and how to expose them on the network. Kubernetes continually works to bring the actual state into line with that configuration.

It schedules containers on nodes with available resources, restarts failed containers, rolls out updates gradually, balances traffic across healthy instances, and manages configuration and secrets.

Pods are the smallest deployable units, usually containing one container each. Deployments and StatefulSets manage groups of Pods; Services provide stable networking.

By abstracting the underlying machines, Kubernetes works across cloud and on-premises environments. It has become the de facto standard for running containerized workloads at scale.
